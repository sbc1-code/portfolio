    WHY THIS CONCEPT

    Give the visitor a useful step before the call.

    Webstacks describes websites as products that evolve with changing journeys, teams, and priorities. This prototype turns that idea into a short decision flow. It separates a visitor’s stated problem from the evidence needed to validate it, suggests the smallest responsible next move, and gives strategy, design, development, and measurement a shared starting point.
