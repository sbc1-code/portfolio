Is your firm ready to go AI-native?

Eight practical questions. One honest starting point. No client or case information required.

5 minutes.

This private browser check looks at workflow, responsibility, information handling, review and measurement.

Short answer

An AI-ready plaintiff firm has a defined workflow, approved information boundaries, accountable human review, trained owners and a baseline for measuring the result. Buying a tool is not the same as changing how work gets done.

Readiness check

Start where the work happens.

This is a planning aid, not legal advice or a compliance determination. Firm leaders should apply the professional rules and requirements that govern their jurisdiction and matters.

Answer-first field guide

Four conditions for responsible adoption.

01 · PURPOSE

Choose a workflow, not a novelty.

Define where AI enters the case lifecycle, what better work looks like and who owns the result. A successful pilot changes a repeatable process instead of adding an isolated shortcut.

Eve: What is an AI-native law firm? ↗
02 · BOUNDARIES

Know what information can enter the system.

Review vendor terms, access controls, retention and training behavior before introducing client information. Confidentiality duties continue to apply when a generative AI tool is used.

ABA Formal Opinion 512 ↗
03 · REVIEW

Keep professional judgment in the loop.

Assign a qualified person to verify output before it shapes advice, a filing, a communication or another material decision. The reviewer remains responsible for the work.

ABA ethics guidance summary ↗
04 · MEASUREMENT

Measure quality with speed.

Record a baseline, review errors and exceptions, and track whether the workflow improves the outcome that justified the change. NIST organizes AI risk work around governing, mapping, measuring and managing.

Metric definitions
  • Mention rate: prompt runs where the answer explicitly names Eve, divided by all runs.
  • Citation rate: prompt runs linking to an eve.legal URL, divided by all runs.
  • Engine coverage: unique answer engines represented in the selected sample.

These are visibility diagnostics. They do not establish lead quality, attribution or revenue.

A repeatable sampling protocol
  1. Freeze a buyer-centered prompt set by intent and practice area.
  2. Record logged-in state, location, engine mode and date.
  3. Run the same prompts on a scheduled cadence without conversational carryover.
  4. Capture mentions, cited domains, cited URLs and answer position.
  5. Review changes against content releases and external events. Do not infer causation from one sample.
Sources & limits

Inspectable by design.

The public-facing assessment and internal measurement workspace are separated so the reader gets a useful answer while the operator gets a repeatable control.

What is implemented

The assessment scores eight explicit answers and returns a staged next-step plan. The measurement workspace supports local data entry, computed visibility metrics, synthetic demonstration records and CSV export. Data is stored only in local browser storage.

What is not claimed

This concept does not represent Eve, provide legal advice, audit a law firm, collect case information or claim measured AI visibility. Synthetic rows are labeled and excluded from any external performance claim. A real launch would require Eve editorial, legal, security, product and analytics review.
