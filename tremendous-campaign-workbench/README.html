Campaign Workbench

Updated by OpenAI Codex · November 6, 2026 Pacific. Refined work sample, approved by Sebastian for publication on November 6, 2026.

Open the workbench. It works as local static files or from a local web server. No installation, API key or account is needed. Browser storage and clipboard permissions depend on the browser; export a copy to retain your work.

What works

Two independent audience drafts, four editable materials each, previews, clipboard copy with text-download fallback, local persistence, review invalidation, reset/cancel, printable HTML campaign export and JSON preparation export. Empty fields block the campaign pack and review mark. Draft packs are labeled as pending human review.

Verification

Core checks passed for both variants, missing fields, review invalidation, escaped HTML, saved-state restoration and corrupt storage recovery. Chromium interaction checks passed for audience switching, reload persistence, all four previews, review/edit invalidation, empty-field blocking, reset/cancel, keyboard tabs, actual HTML/JSON downloads and clipboard content. Tested at 1440×1000, 390×844 and 320×700; no horizontal overflow. Blocked storage retained in-session edits and displayed its limitation. Runtime error capture was clean. Campaign export PDFs were rendered and visually inspected. Physical devices, Safari and screen readers were not tested. No production or business results are claimed.
