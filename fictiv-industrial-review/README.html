Public research, ready for a reviewable handoff

Independent work sample for Fictiv / MISUMI Americas
Prepared by Sebastian Becerra with OpenAI Codex · November 5, 2026

For acquisition and lifecycle teammates deciding which industrial accounts merit further investigation, and why. The working output is an editable, source-linked research record and printable brief—not a qualified opportunity.

Try it

  1. Select an account and inspect its public facts, source dates and evidence limits.
  2. Review the saved AI analysis; correct the proposed need, segment, priority or next action.
  3. For an eligible record, enter a reviewer label and confirm the source/inference review. Approve the version, then download its CSV or printable HTML brief.
  4. Edit an approved field: approval is removed. Switch accounts or reload to check independent persistence. Reset restores only the selected account.

Held and excluded accounts cannot become approved campaign exports. Their evidence briefs remain downloadable with that status, so the team can retain the reason for not proceeding.

Why this is useful

Each handoff preserves the company, sector, signal, research priority, proposed segment, relevant Fictiv resource, next action and open questions. A teammate can trace an inference to its source, revise it and export the version actually reviewed. The hypothesis is that this reduces research handoff ambiguity; no preparation-time, conversion, pipeline or revenue improvement has been measured.

Connection to my work

At Los Bravos, I turned manufacturer and nearshoring research into prioritized lists, company briefs and a searchable lead workspace. That company–sector–signal–priority–notes structure informs this sample. No client contacts, private lists or internal notes are reused.

At SeeScan, I built cross-site measurement and scheduled reporting with defined metrics, freshness checks and data-quality controls. BorderPulse reflects my work building and operating a product with official public data and automated updates. Those experiences inform the emphasis on sources, durable state and a usable output. This demonstration does not establish enterprise CRM/CDP ownership or scaled lifecycle experimentation.

Actual AI assistance, with clear limits

I selected industrial manufacturers and precomputed AI analysis, reviewed the first working account and directed the six-account expansion. OpenAI Codex researched public sources, authored the saved analyses, implemented the interface and ran the checks. My direction and early review are distinct from the assistant’s execution.

The analyses were generated in the preparation conversation and checked against the linked sources by Codex. There was no separate model API job, and no model is called when this application opens or changes. The preparation record retains source summaries, the task instruction, actual saved outputs, assistant checks and review scope. Later local edits retain before/after values and approval actions.

No individual account has been pre-approved for campaign use. Local reviewer labels are self-entered, not authenticated. Approval in this demo does not authorize outreach, certify technical suitability or establish Sebastian’s application-package review.

What this does not do

No CRM connection, contact enrichment, messages, live generation, keys, tracking or remote persistence. It is not a Fictiv system. Source links open only when selected. Actual activation would require validated customer/CRM data, permission, suppression rules, identity resolution, engineering qualification and measurement design.

Sources were checked November 5, 2026 and may change. Announcement dates and page-check dates are separate; announced plans are not represented as completed projects. Fictiv customer results are not attributed to these accounts or to this sample.

Local operation

All application files are static and use relative paths. Reviews are saved only in the current browser. When storage is blocked, edits work for the session and the interface explains the limit. Reset affects only the selected account. CSV protects spreadsheet formula prefixes and preserves quoted text/newlines; HTML escapes edited text. These controls support a local demonstration, not an enterprise audit log.
