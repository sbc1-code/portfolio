This calculator estimates changes in cost to serve. Revenue, customer experience and risk require separate measurement; none is assigned a dollar value here.

How are the numbers calculated?

Baseline human hours = new issues × (1 + human repeat rate) × handling time ÷ 60.

With AI, direct-human and handed-off issues retain that human repeat rate. AI-contained issues that return create one human follow-up, with no further repeat in this simplified model. Every handoff also adds the extra minutes you specify.

Positive capacity value × your cash-conversion share = avoided human spend. Additional human cost is counted at 100%. Subtract AI usage, monthly program costs, and one-time setup to get year-one cash impact.

Payback uses steady monthly benefit. Routing break-even varies only the fraction sent to AI, holding handoffs, repeat contacts and all costs constant.

What is intentionally missing?

Ramp-up, seasonality, staffing schedules, minimum staffing levels, contract tiers, discounting, taxes, and later repeat-contact chains. Fixed program costs apply even at zero routing. Each initial issue can create at most one repeat contact. The model assumes any human follow-up completes the modeled journey.

“No repeat in window” is not the same as successful resolution. Validate first-contact resolution, customer satisfaction, abandonment, compliance, and escalation quality separately before expanding a pilot.

Where does Cresta fit?

Cresta’s platform includes AI Agent, Agentic Assist, Deputy Agent and Conversation Intelligence. This concept focuses on an AI-first service journey; it does not predict performance for any Cresta product.

Cresta’s own guidance favors resolution quality over containment alone and full program cost over license-only math. The next step is validating your assumptions, not treating this result as a quote.
