Publishing workflow

The work is not just the page. It is the handoff from request to verified production.

1 · IntakeClarify brief, audience, owner and launch date.
2 · StageImplement copy, imagery and components in CMS.
3 · RefineUse HTML/CSS when the component needs a precise fix.
4 · ValidateCheck responsive behavior, links and targeting logic.
5 · LaunchPublish with production verification and clear handoff.
6 · MonitorUse analytics and feedback to catch issues and improve.
