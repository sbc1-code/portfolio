# Customer Friction Loop

A bounded product-operations proof built against the public responsibilities in Ambrook's Product Operations listing.

## What it demonstrates

- Synthetic customer signals clustered into repeatable friction patterns
- Evidence scoring and right-sized opportunity selection
- A papercut fix tied to a measurable customer outcome
- An evidence-gated agentic workflow with a human exception path
- A two-day experiment memo and explicit scale/revise/stop rules

## Product decision

Ship a narrow experiment that exposes verified payment state and uses the same evidence to answer routine status questions. Do not automate exceptions or rebuild the broader payments surface until the experiment proves value.

## Scope and privacy

All signals, customers, transaction states, metrics, and outputs are synthetic. No Ambrook customer, financial, product telemetry, or internal company data is used.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are required.
