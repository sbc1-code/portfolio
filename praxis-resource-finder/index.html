SOURCE DETAILS
FIRST WORKING EDITION

Useful information.
A shorter path to it.

A small, working resource finder for colleagues getting oriented to published Praxis information. Six curated sources and three starting tasks make the concept reviewable.

How it was made

Sebastian Becerra directed the concept and scope. AI assisted source research, implementation and testing. This independent prototype demonstrates resource discovery, not pharmaceutical-platform administration.

What it does

Matches search words and a small set of curated aliases to source titles, descriptions and topics. It returns resources, not generated answers.

What it knows

The six linked pages were checked on November 5, 2026. Summaries are independently written; publication dates are shown only when stated. This is a frozen snapshot and does not refresh automatically.

Privacy & limits

No accounts, analytics, uploads, persistent search history or network calls for search. Opening a source leaves this prototype and follows that website’s policies. Do not enter personal or medical information. No claim of HIPAA certification or legal compliance is made.

Public availability does not establish internal approval for promotional use. No individual medical advice, current regulatory determination or measured time savings are claimed.
