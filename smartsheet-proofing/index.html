From request to client decision

One proof. A clear review process.

Invite your client to the proof.

Open the proof and choose Invite. Enter your client’s email address and send a formal review request.

You can invite a reviewer who is not shared to the project sheet.

Use a review invitation for an approval decision. An @mention follows a different sharing process.

How to request review and approval

The review link opens the proof.

Your client follows Review Proof from the notification to an external review page. They can comment and annotate without being shared to the underlying sheet.

A Smartsheet account is required. A client without one is prompted to create a free account before viewing the proof.

What an invited reviewer needs to know

Approve or request changes.

An invited reviewer selects Approve or Requires Changes. The proof records responses and shows who is still pending.

A decision is different from Review Complete.

The sheet Owner, Admin or Editor can mark the proof complete to close comments and version uploads. That does not mean every reviewer approved it.

About this independent work

This is Sebastian Becerra’s proposed improvement to a Creative Operations marketing section. It explains existing Proofing behavior using official public documentation and images. It is not a live Smartsheet product, a permission check or an endorsed Smartsheet page. Nothing is sent or recorded.

How this was made. Sebastian selected and directed this work. Codex assisted with research, copy, HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation and local testing. Playwright and Chromium were used for functional checks and screenshots. The decisions and final work remain Sebastian’s responsibility. Sebastian reviewed and approved this sample for publication.

Official images illustrate different documented examples, not one continuous recorded session. The request email and external recipient screen were unavailable in the verified visual set. This independent prototype does not send proofing invitations, review decisions or analytics.
